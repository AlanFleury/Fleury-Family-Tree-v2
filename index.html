Merge duplicate person IDs

Choose the person ID to keep and the duplicate ID to merge into it. The keeper's ID remains unchanged. Blank fields are filled from the duplicate; notes and sources are combined. Conflicting non-empty fields keep the keeper's value, and the original duplicate record plus relationship snapshot are preserved in private merge history. All relationships are redirected, duplicate links are removed, and the merge is staged for review before anything is saved online.
