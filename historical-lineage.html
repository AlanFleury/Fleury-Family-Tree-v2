About this register

This page brings together the historical Fleury research assembled for the family archive. It is a working genealogical register, not a claim that every branch or descendant has been proved or that the family is complete to the present day.

Evidence key: Recorded in cited account Probable / inferred Unverified / conflicting. A historical genealogy is not the same as a primary record. Dates and relationships should be checked against parish registers, civil certificates, wills, deeds and contemporary clerical records before being treated as proven.

The French La Raffinière genealogy and the Irish Huguenot genealogy are presented as separate lines. No documentary connection between medieval Michel Fleury and the Huguenot Rev. Louis Fleury has yet been established.

Part I — The French family of La Raffinière

1. Michel Fleury, Seigneur de la Raffinière Recorded 1413

Michel is recorded making homage for La Raffinière on 3 August and 1 September 1413. These are record dates, not an established birth date.

  • Wife: unknown. A proposed daughter of Pierre Joubert, Seigneur de Fayolle, is only a hypothesis.
  • Probable son: Jean Fleury, Seigneur de la Raffinière.

2. Jean Fleury, Seigneur de la Raffinière

Recorded in 1442. Wife not established. A further Jean Fleury is given as his probable son.

3. Jean Fleury, Seigneur de la Raffinière

Recorded in 1491 and still living in 1556. Married Jacquette Lauvergnat, from the family of the Lords of Langes, probably around 1480.

Children identified: Hugues Fleury and Philippe Fleury (founder of the Vert branch). The source does not exclude additional children.

4. Hugues Fleury, Seigneur de la Raffinière

Married Isabeau (also Esther) de Parthenay, daughter of Jean de Parthenay, Seigneur de Langevinière, and Huguette de Voullon; probably around 1504. She died before 1 March 1525.

Children named: Mathurin, Louis, Renée and Henriette Fleury.

5. Mathurin Fleury

Seigneur de la Brande, du Marchais and des Fosses. Married Anne Furgon in 1531.

Children: Charles Fleury and Claude Fleury.

6A. Charles Fleury, Seigneur de la Brande

Married Louise Lingier. Daughter identified: Judith Fleury, who married Léon Béranger, Seigneur de la Brande. A 1675 legal agreement distinguishes this Charles from another Charles Fleury, Sieur des Brandes; do not merge the two families.

6B. Claude Fleury, Seigneur de la Raffinière

Married Louise des Francs on 24 August 1561. Child identified: Gabriel de Fleury.

7. Gabriel de Fleury

First wife Louise de La Barre, married 30 January 1601; child identified: Jacques de Fleury. Second wife Nicole Regnauld, married 16 August 1620. No further children are named in the account.

8. Jacques de Fleury

First wife Marie de Mallevau, married 18 October 1628; no children recorded from this marriage. Second wife Susanne du Fay, married 6 January 1644.

Children: Jean de Fleury and Méry de Fleury (naval service).

9. Jean de Fleury

Marriages: Madeleine Régnier de La Planche (18 June 1664); Susanne de Goret (16 February 1683); Jeanne Bérard (1696).

Children by Madeleine: Olivier, Elisabeth, Susanne and Madeleine de Fleury.

  • Elisabeth married Charles de Bessac, Seigneur de la Feuilleterie, on 19 February 1688.
  • Susanne married Pierre Garnier, Seigneur de Morice.
  • Madeleine married first Pierre Pandin, Seigneur des Jarriges, then Antoine de Choisy, Seigneur de Marraux.

Children by Susanne de Goret: Joachim de Fleury (Beauregard branch) and Pierre de Fleury (Lhoumède branch). No children are named from the Jeanne Bérard marriage.

10. Olivier de Fleury (baptised 19 April 1676; died 11 December 1704)

Married Jacquette Françoise Cacault on 31 October 1696.

Children recorded: Madeleine Françoise; Marie Anne (born 17 October 1698; married René Charles de Brouillac on 24 April 1724); Olivier Jean Baptiste (died young); Marguerite Julie (born 31 March 1701; married Louis Augron); Jean-Louis de Fleury (born 25 September 1702); and Marie Monique (baptised 3 May 1704).

11. Jean-Louis de Fleury

Born 25 September 1702. Married Madeleine Jourdain de Boistillé on 28 February 1729.

Children recorded: Gabriel Philippe; Marie Madeleine (baptised 7 January 1737); Madeleine Julie (married Charles César Desmier, Marquis de Chenon, in 1757, then François, Comte de Saluces, in 1763); and Charles (buried 4 October 1738).

Other French branches

  • Philippe Fleury, brother of Hugues: founder of the Vert branch.
  • Joachim de Fleury, son of Jean de Fleury and Susanne de Goret: founder of the Beauregard branch.
  • Pierre de Fleury, son of Jean de Fleury and Susanne de Goret: founder of the Lhoumède branch.

The complete descendants of Vert, Beauregard and Lhoumède have not yet been transcribed into this register. Their absence here does not mean those branches ended.

Part II — The Huguenot and Irish line

1. Rev. Pierre Fleury

Protestant minister at Preuilly, Touraine. Married Charlotte Dubourdieu. Son identified: Rev. Louis Fleury. Further children have not been established in the account consulted.

2. Rev. Louis Fleury

Protestant minister; historic accounts associate him with Tours, Chauvigny and Saint-Lô, although his exact appointments and final French ministry require original-record verification.

Married Esther (Ester) Dubourdieu at Terchant on 18 September 1678. Esther was born 18 December 1656, daughter of Olivier Dubourdieu and Marie de Gennes. Louis and Esther were cousins through the Dubourdieu family.

Children named in the DuBourdieu family history:

  1. Philip Amaury Fleury
  2. Amury Fleury
  3. Esther Fleury
  4. Marie Fleury

Family denization in London is recorded on 9 April 1687. Louis later served as chaplain to William of Orange and minister at Leiden from 1689. After Esther's death, he married Marie le Clerc; no children from that second marriage are identified in the source. Louis died in 1707.

Chronology conflict: Philip is reported born in 1671, before Louis and Esther's 1678 marriage. Some accounts give a 1683 flight from France while the family history gives other dates. Do not silently resolve these discrepancies.

3. Philip Amaury (Amauret) Fleury

Reported born 1671; educated at Leiden; served as a French Protestant minister in London and then in Ireland. Ordained by Bishop Henry Compton in 1697 according to the academic and historical accounts; served the United French Churches in Ireland from 1716 and was chaplain to Lord Arran. He was also associated with Colonel La Bouchétière's regiment.

Child identified: Rev. Antoine Fleury. Philip's wife, death date and any other children have not been established.

4. Rev. Antoine Fleury

Educated at Leiden and ordained there on 4 September 1728. Settled in Ireland; became Vicar Choral of Lismore in 1761. Married into the de Rochebrune family. Later accounts call his wife Marie Julie de Rochebrune; her full name, parentage and marriage date remain to be confirmed from original records.

Child identified: Rev. George Lewis / George Louis Fleury.

5. Rev. George Louis Fleury (1740 or 1741–1825)

Prebendary of Kilgobinet and Archdeacon of Waterford from 1773; died 27 October 1825. Married Isabella Weekes on 7 October 1785 at St Patrick's Church, Waterford. Isabella is reported as 1771–1838.

Nine children recorded:

  1. Richard Chenevix Fleury (1786–1854)
  2. Henry Francis Downes Fleury (1788–1861)
  3. Elizabeth Melesina Fleury (1790–1853)
  4. George Alexander Fleury (1793–1829)
  5. Robert Antoine Fleury (1795–1853)
  6. Isabella Julia Fleury (1797–1873)
  7. Charles Marlay Fleury (1802–1863)
  8. Mary Anne Fleury (1806–1884)
  9. John Franquefort Fleury (1811–1858)

Part III — Descendants of George Louis and Isabella Weekes

A. Richard Chenevix Fleury (1786–1854)

Birth and death are recorded. A reliable spouse and child list has not yet been established.

B. Henry Francis Downes Fleury (1788–1861)

A family-tree contribution names Ellen Maria Colclough Cook as his wife. The marriage and any children need confirmation from contemporary records. No children are asserted here as established.

C. Elizabeth Melesina Fleury (1790–1853) — McClintock branch

Married Henry McClintock at Waterford on 14 December 1809. The McClintock family history records fourteen children, twelve surviving to adulthood. Identified children include Isabella Marion, Louisa, Emily Caroline, Francis Leopold and Alfred Henry; the shorter lists previously circulated are incomplete.

Sir Francis Leopold McClintock (1819–1907)

Married Annette Elizabeth Dunlop on 12 October 1870. Children recorded: Henry Foster McClintock (1871–1959); Anna Elizabeth (1873–1957), married Bernard Eyre Greenwell; John William Leopold (1874–1929); Robert Singleton (1876–1967); and Elizabeth Florence Mary (1882–1913).

Dr Alfred Henry McClintock (1821–1881)

Married Frances Cuppaidge on 2 May 1848. Children recorded: Elizabeth Florence (1849–1866); Katherine Elizabeth (born 1851), married Charles James Maxwell Lefroy in 1872; Leopold Alfred (1853–1933); Frances Edith (born 1856), married Edmund F. B. Wilson in 1890; James Frederick Foster (1859–1920), married Clara Elizabeth Charlotte Adams in 1893; and Caroline Florence (1867–1927), married Sir William Longfield Brady.

James Frederick Foster McClintock

Children with Clara Elizabeth Charlotte Adams: Melesina F. (born 1894; married Ernest A. Phipps in 1917); Clara Patricia (born 1898; death details require checking); Geoffrey (born 1900); and Fanny (born 1902).

D. George Alexander Fleury (1793–1829)

Spouse and children not established in the material reviewed.

E. Robert Antoine Fleury (1795–1853)

Spouse and children not established in the material reviewed.

F. Isabella Julia Fleury (1797–1873) — Ryland branch

Married Rev. Richard Hopkins Ryland on 7 August 1818 at St Patrick's Church, Waterford. The Waterford account says they had eight children (six sons and two daughters). Names identified in the accounts include Richard, George Henry, Charles Thomas, Rev. John Frederick Ryland (1822–1896) and Theodore Ryland (1827–1904). The five names are not a complete list.

Theodore Ryland (1827–1904)

Married Mary Anne Hughes. Son identified: Richard Theodore Hughes Ryland (1871–1958). His spouse and children are not established in the sources reviewed.

G. Charles Marlay Fleury (1802–1863)

Clergyman and chaplain in Dublin. Children identified in historical accounts include Charles William Fleury, chaplain of Molyneux Chapel from 1890 to 1908, and Louis Arthur Fleury, a medical practitioner in Montevideo. Their spouses, other siblings and descendants are not yet established.

H. Mary Anne Fleury (1806–1884)

Some historical wording has been interpreted as linking a Mary Anne Fleury to Rev. Richard Ryland. Later family records identify Isabella Julia as his wife. This apparent conflict is not resolved here; a second marriage should not be invented.

I. John Franquefort Fleury (1811–1858) — Eccles / Stillorgan branch

Married Albina Newman. Their son Rev. Louis Richard Fleury (c.1851–1928) married Alice Dora Eccles on 12 April 1877 in Cork. Alice was a daughter of Robert Gilbert Eccles and died in 1921.

Fifteen children are recorded:

  3. Renee Isabel Annie Fleury (1880–1890)

  6. Constance de Partherny Fleury (c.1884–; further details unknown)
  7. Stella du Bourdieu Fleury (c.1885–1890)
  8. Richard Chenevix Fleury (1887–1941)
  9. Amoret Louis de Rochebrune Fleury (c.1889–; further details unknown)
  10. Gabrielle Grace Fleury (c.1890–; further details unknown)
  11. Robert Leopold McClintock Fleury (1892–1915; killed at Gallipoli)
  12. Aimee Esther Fleury (c.1894–1924; died in Victoria, Canada)
  13. John Charles Fleury (1895–1916; died in France)
  14. Hugo Valentine Fleury (1897–1917; died in France)
  15. Claire Marguerite Fleury (c.1900–; further details unknown)

Louis Claude Robert Fleury (1879–1957)

Married Ethel Maude Exell on 6 June 1921 at Shanklin, Isle of Wight. Their daughter Renée Alice Fleury married William George Hutchinson on 11 July 1952 at St Brigid's Church, Stillorgan.

Richard Chenevix Fleury (1887–1941)

Married Dr Geraldine Murphy, who died in 1939. Richard died in 1941. No children are identified in the family account.

Part IV — Other Irish Fleury references not yet securely attached

  • Anthony Fleury, minister at Portarlington, is named as father of John Charles Fleury, Dublin physician and obstetrician (died 29 September 1797 aged 64). His exact connection to the Antoine–George Louis line is not proven.
  • Rev. Richard Fleury, Rector of Dunmore East, is named among descendants of Archdeacon George Louis Fleury. Wife and children not yet identified.
  • Rev. Charles Fleury, ordained by the Archbishop of Tuam on 21 January 1827. Do not confuse him with Charles Marlay Fleury; parentage, spouse and children need verification.
  • Rev. John Devereux Fleury, named as Librarian of Waterford Cathedral. Exact parentage and descendants not established.
